## Description

The completed Medium web game exposes a fourth-down urgency gap: down nine
with Q4 3:37 remaining, fourth-and-five at its own 16, the CPU punts. The
2–5-minute desperation branch handles short yardage, helpful field goals and
opponent territory, but otherwise falls through to the conservative policy.
It does not assess the remaining possessions needed to erase the deficit.
Matching public-2026 Medium probes returned punt for all 100 seeds. This is
a specific follow-up to the broader AI-003/AI-006 observations, not a claim
that every late own-territory fourth down must be attempted.

## Resolution / verification

Define a fourth-down urgency assessment using score deficit, remaining time,
field position, distance and timeout budgets. Add failing synthetic/public-2026
regressions before changing policy. Cover the observed multi-score situation,
nearby one-score situations, earlier-game conservative controls, short-yardage
choices and useful field goals. Verify real-engine clock and possession
consequences through the web/CLI paths. Require play-test confirmation before
closure; completion or a favorable final score alone is insufficient.
