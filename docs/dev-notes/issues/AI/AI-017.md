## Description

## Resolution / verification

--- 2026-11-14 00:00 ---
Fixed the made-field-goal branch of `ComputerAI.should_accept_penalty`. When the
defense is offended and an offensive foul was flagged, the CPU now enforces the
foul and replays the kick instead of keeping the made three points. The kicking
team still keeps a made field goal whenever it is the offended side, and a
missed kick keeps its previous policy. When several offensive fouls are
available, the defense enforces the longest and reports that option's original
index, so the projected longer re-kick is the one applied.

Tests first: `tests/test_ai_field_goal_penalty.py` builds real pending decisions
from a synthetic field-goal chart on the public 2026 sample teams. Four cases
failed against the unchanged AI for both possessions: defense enforcing a foul
on a made kick (the synthetic return stayed in the score), and choosing the
longest of several fouls at its original index. Controls that already behaved correctly pass: offense
keeping a made kick after a defensive foul, defense keeping a missed kick, and
offense taking a defensive foul after a miss.

Verified after review: 24 focused cases pass; 608 tests passed across the
field-goal, AI, penalty, clock-flow and accounting suites; Ruff clean. Documentation updated in docs/ai-difficulty.md. Play-test confirmation
and full regression remain before closing.

--- 2026-11-14 00:00 ---
Code review found that always enforcing could reject a guaranteed win: defense
leading 6-0 with 0:03 left in Q4, a made kick carrying OFF 5 ended the game 6-3,
but enforcing it left an untimed down from which the offense could attempt a
winning touchdown instead of re-kicking. The review also asked for engine-owned
field-goal penalty projections instead of assuming erasing points always helps.

Fixed with that projection. `PaydirtGameEngine.project_field_goal_penalty_choice`
runs the real `apply_fg_penalty_decision` on a throwaway copy and returns the
resulting score, spot, down, distance, period, clock, game-over and untimed-down
state, or `None` for an ineligible choice. The clock charge moved to
`_field_goal_decision_seconds`, which the projection pins to each documented
range's low end. A projection restores the global dice state and cannot change
live state, statistics or the play log, including for blocked and snap-fumble
kicks that replay recovery rules. `ComputerAI` now keeps the made kick when the
projected retained kick wins the game and enforcement does not; with time left,
or when the kick is tied or trailing, it still enforces the longest foul.

Tests first: six new cases failed before the production change - the terminal
win for both possessions and the four projection cases (absent API, both
side-effect checks, and the non-field-goal `None` control). The controls prove
the guard is narrow: 2:00 left still enforces, a made kick that loses anyway
still enforces, and non-terminal projections expose the enforced spot and clock.
Blocked and snap-fumble projection cases prove recovery dice and statistics are
untouched. No existing expectation was weakened.
