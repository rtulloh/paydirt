## Description

Hard stores a full own-team analyzer but ordinary defensive selection does not
consult its defensive chart or the opponent offense chart. Its model uses
observed run/pass tendencies and game state. Ordinary offense uses its own
offense chart and history, without opposing defensive strengths. Configuration
and learning are present; these missing chart responses are a separate capability
gap from AI-025's observation-recording fix and AI-019's situational ordering.

Both home/away factories and CLI/web/auto adapters are traced. Controlled public
2026/synthetic snapshots hold state, tendencies and RNG fixed, and independently
verify meaningful chart contrasts through the real priority/resolver distribution.
Six requirement assertions fail: changing a uniquely stronger defensive formation
A to F leaves C selected; reversing opponent offense strength leaves C selected;
reversing opponent defense strength leaves all nine offensive weights at 1/9.
Six own-offense/tendency/no-hidden-dice controls pass.

## Resolution / verification

Review and implement public chart-informed matchup utilities and explicit priors
without reading the pending opponent call or future dice. Preserve physical mass,
core priority semantics, unresolved-outcome disclosure, situational coaching and
variation. Add self-contained public/synthetic regressions before the production
correction, covering all three responses and both team sides, no hidden call/dice,
cache identity, preview purity, existing situational behavior and meaningful
unresolved/turnover controls. Renew relevant full-game exploitation, repetition
and policy/actual-season evidence; do not close from stationary probes alone.
No new frequency cap, source target change, chart mutation or BB rule assumption.

See [Hard AI chart-response baseline](../../hard-ai-chart-response-baseline.md)
for exact data flow, controls, limitations and the correction questions.
