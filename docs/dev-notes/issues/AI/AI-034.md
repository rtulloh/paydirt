## Description

Easy and Medium previously shared Hard's exact fourth-down field-goal judgement. Aggression changed how often each mode went for it, but every difficulty read the kicking team's chart probability with the same precision, so a marginal kick was evaluated identically and none of the lower modes showed the bounded judgement errors their difficulty implies. Implementation in progress: CoachingPrecision now adds Easy/Medium optimism and band confusion around the field-goal decision thresholds, with its own decision RNG; Hard and the legacy balanced auto baseline remain exact.

## Resolution / verification

- Easy deviates more often than Medium on a seeded grid of marginal fourth-down kicks; Hard and profile-less ComputerAI never deviate.
- Confusion only fires within the documented band margin, optimism is bounded, and obvious kicks or punts never flip.
- Endgame policies (last-second tie/win, end-of-half, urgent multi-score, victory formation, Hail Mary) ignore precision noise.
- The precision stream does not consume or alter gameplay dice, is reproducible from a controlled seed, and create_computer_ai wires Easy/Medium/Hard correctly.
- Public-sample AI audits at all three difficulties complete every game; review and record fourth-down call shares, field-goal attempts and scores without tuning chart strength.
- Document the precision model, its bounds and the chart-based fourth-down bands in docs/ai-difficulty.md and docs/seasons.md.
