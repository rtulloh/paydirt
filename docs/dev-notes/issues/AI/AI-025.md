## Description

The chart-confidence repeated-long-pass probe finds that auto-mode Hard's
opponent tracker remains empty. The production auto-game loop resolves penalties
and observes selected outcomes but never calls record_opponent_play. Interactive
CLI and web modes do. Therefore existing auto-mode Hard-vs-Hard campaigns do not
exercise learning during a game, even when all counts and final scores reconcile.
The separate audit harness has no compensating learning hook. An exploratory
Hard-defense probe heavily selects SPREAD from its unlearned model; it cannot
establish an adaptive defense's response to the repeated call.

## Resolution / verification

Record the observed selected call in the defending AI's model exactly once after
penalty resolution, using the preplay down/distance and original offense even
after turnovers or score/kickoff chains. Match the existing interactive observed-
call convention: accepted and offsetting fouls may reveal a selected call to the
coach, while those nullified plays still contribute no official box statistics.
Let the existing tendency classifier exclude punts/field goals. Default balanced
AI has no learner and must retain its behavior. Keep coaching learning separate
from the read-only measurement callbacks and source-statistics counters.

Twelve new public-fixture cases fail first (both sides, run/pass, accepted,
declined, offsetting and interception outcomes); four special-call controls pass.
The implementation and renewed policy campaigns are pending. Old reports retain
their actual runtime identity but cannot certify a corrected adaptive policy.
