## Description

Found in the 2026-11-13 review and confirmed to predate the AI-002 change. In the defense-offended branch of `should_accept_penalty`, the engine projection deliberately returns no situations when the retained play ends the drive, so the branch falls back to the legacy yardage test. That fallback accepts the foul whenever the retained play gained any yardage, even when the gain failed to convert on fourth down. Example with public 2026 sample teams: fourth-and-10 at midfield, a three-yard gain, and OFF 5. The projection correctly detects a turnover on downs, but the CPU enforces the foul and the offense keeps the ball on fourth-and-15 instead of punting or turning it over. AI-002 documents that fourth-down stops keep their existing policy, which is true of the buggy existing policy, and its guard test covers only an incompletion.

## Resolution / verification

- Add failing synthetic regressions for fourth-down gains that fall short with an available offensive foul, including a zero gain and a positive gain, and assert the CPU takes possession.
- Make the fallback preserve a stopped drive rather than testing only for positive yardage, without weakening the AI-001 first-down comparisons or the AI-002 new coverages.
- Record the corrected behaviour in docs/ai-difficulty.md and confirm it in an AI play-test audit.
