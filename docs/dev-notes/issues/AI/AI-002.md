## Description

On an incomplete first-down pass, CPU declines a 15-yard offensive penalty: second-and-ten instead of first-and-25.

## Resolution / verification

The offended defense now compares the retained play with each engine-projected
enforcement instead of testing only whether the play gained positive yardage.
Situations are ranked by the yards the offense still must cover to reach the
defense's goal, then by the down, then by the distance to convert, so actual
enforcement (including half-distance) is priced and the play is kept unless
enforcement is strictly better. Scores, safeties, turnovers, fumbles, return
chains, fourth-down stops and requested spikes keep their existing policy.
Synthetic/public regressions live in `tests/test_ai_penalty_defense.py`;
`docs/ai-difficulty.md` and `docs/development.md` describe the change.

--- 2026-11-13 21:10 ---
Implemented the defensive comparison in the core/CPU path. The engine projects
the retained-play situation and each offensive option's enforced situation on a
shallow state copy with the same `advance_ball`, `next_down` and `resolve_penalty`
routines `apply_penalty_decision` uses, without changing live state,
statistics, call logs or dice. `PlayOutcome.retained_play_situation` and
`penalty_situations` now serve the defense when an ordinary scrimmage play draws
an offensive foul; AI-001's first-down comparison is unchanged and still reads
the same fields on the offense-offended branch.

The CPU ranks options by `100 - ball_position`, then down, then distance to
convert. Field position leads because an offensive foul is enforced from the
previous spot (or half the distance to the offense's own goal), so the only way
the retained play wins is a retained loss larger than the foul. A retained
score, safety, turnover, fumble, return chain, fourth-down stop or requested
spike is still outside the comparison and keeps the earlier policy. On a tie the
play is kept, so no equivalent situation is discarded.

Baseline before the change: 16 of 24 new synthetic/public cases failed only
because no projection existed (`retained_play_situation` was `None`); the eight
policy guards (spike, projected touchdown, retained safety, fourth-down stop,
plus sidedness fixtures) already passed. Added `tests/test_ai_penalty_defense.py`
(24 cases): the recorded first-and-25 versus second-and-10 case at three starting
downs and both home/away sides, sack-versus-foul both ways, half-distance
enforcement at the own 3, multiple fouls with an ineligible defensive option,
real priority-chart rerolls, preview/enforcement agreement, and the guards above.

Changed existing expectations, with reasons:
- `tests/test_chart_reference_contexts.py`: an all-foul chart at own 40 previously
  recorded four erased offensive plays because the defense declined every
  incompletion; the defense now enforces four 5-yard fouls (own 20, first-and-30)
  and no attempt is recorded.
- `paydirt-web/backend/tests/test_pending_penalty_scoring.py`: the goal-line
  case flagged the human as defense while the live possession was the human's,
  so the route let the CPU auto-decide and the "ball stays at the 99" assertion
  only restated the old decline policy. The scenario now makes the human the
  actual defender, which preserves the original no-inferred-touchdown check.

Local play-test evidence (ignored `.ai-audits/ai-002/`): 224 complete
Hard-vs-Hard generated regular-season games on public 1983 charts. The observer
recorded 1,177 projected defensive decisions: 1,156 enforced the foul, 21 kept
the play, and 440 differed from the old yardage-only policy; every applied
alternative matched its projection (0 mismatches) and every decision matched the
stated rule (0 mismatches). The remaining 327 defensive decisions stayed
unprojected by design: 224 special/untimed plays, 44 turnovers, 42 projected
touchdowns, 9 fumbles, 8 retained safety/turnover-on-downs. The 2026 sample
season added 16 more complete games (78 projected decisions, 15 changed).

Known limits: this is a coaching ordering, not an expected-points estimate, so
near-equivalent late-down situations can still choose either option defensibly.
The public-season and local-season AI audits and the short game/all-games runners
passed with reconciled totals. The released-chart gate already failed at HEAD
for this release train (unmodified `play_resolver.py`, `save_game.py`,
`game_state.py`, `interactive_game.py` and `routes.py` also mismatch the
immutable v1.10.3 baseline), so this change joins the pending audited paired
baseline-extension route rather than claiming that gate passes. Full core and
backend suites pass apart from one pre-existing environment failure in
`test_frozen_launcher`; frontend unit tests hit local `localStorage` stand-ins
that fail with 12 pre-existing environment errors. A browser E2E attempt on
2026-11-13 passed 17 checks and failed `full-games.spec.js` in the human
defensive-timeout request assertion (`call_defensive_timeout` sent false), an
area this change does not touch. Re-run the full pre-push regression, including
that browser game, before closing.
