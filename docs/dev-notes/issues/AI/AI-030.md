## Description

Updated 2024 charts improve historical qualifier overlap from 9.05 to 10.01 of
14 teams, but Kansas City's playoff frequency falls from 79.69% to 35.55%.
Baltimore and Detroit reach 51.56% and 47.27% of simulated Super Bowls.
These are diagnostic questions, not demonstrated team-specific bugs.

Chart-focused calibration and Hard seasons also show substantial completion
differences: Kansas City 65.97% versus 58.75%, Baltimore 68.03% versus 60.17%,
and Detroit 71.12% versus 63.08%. Their chart CSVs match, but measurement policy
and end-zone interception-return settings differ. Broad runtime fingerprints
differ through source-generation files; inspected gameplay files match. The
existing evidence cannot assign the completion difference solely to Hard AI.

## Resolution / verification

Run a bounded comparison holding gameplay code, charts, schedule, seeds, return
preferences and accounting definitions fixed. Vary only coaching policy and
measure call mix, defense choices, outcomes and uncertainty. Separate regular
season strength, division competition and playoff byes from postseason variance.
Use source efficiency targets rather than restoring a team's historical wins;
Kansas City's updated passing YPA already matches its source target. Add failing
synthetic/public tests only if a specific policy defect is demonstrated, and
require play-test confirmation before closure.
