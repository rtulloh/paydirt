## Description

`paydirt/interactive_game.py:2713-2715` selects the CPU's conversion play with `cpu_ai.select_offense(game)` before `attempt_two_point` moves the ball, so the policy sees the post-touchdown state (ball at 97 after a return touchdown, leftover down and distance, live clock); only PUNT and FIELD_GOAL are remapped. Synthetic reproduction after a defensive pick-six: HOME leading 9-8 with 18 seconds left, `cpu_should_go_for_two` True, `select_offense` returns **QB_KNEEL** (Victory Formation) and the try is "NO GOOD (qb_kneel for -2 yards)". Trailing 6-8 with 8 seconds left the policy returns FIELD_GOAL (End-of-Half FG), masked by the guard into LINE_PLUNGE. After a scrimmage touchdown the ball is at 100 so the kneel guard blocks QB_KNEEL, but the selection is still made on the wrong phase. The try defense is always STANDARD (`:2715`, `:2923`). `should_go_for_two` also returns False whenever `game_over` is set (`computer_ai.py:806`), so a touchdown that expires the clock while trailing by two always kicks the extra point.

## Resolution / verification

- Give the AI an explicit conversion-phase observation (two-point try from the 2, no clock, success = 2 points) and select from scrimmage plays only; the defense selects a formation for the try.
- Tests: victory-formation and end-of-half branches never fire on a try; trailing by two at 0:00 with a try pending still goes for two when the rules allow.
