## Description

`ComputerAI._fourth_down_decision()` applies fixed 1983 assumptions to every season: an easy kick
is at most 35 yards, a makeable kick is at most 47, and the long-kick boundary is 55. The method's
docstring and comments explicitly describe 1983 coaching and success rates, but a 2024 game uses
the same thresholds even though long-distance kicking improved substantially after 1983.

The decision also uses the difficulty aggression value (`0.3`, `0.5`, or `0.7`) rather than the
season's existing `ai_behavior.strategic.fourth_down_aggression` value. As a result, changing the
era profile does not change this core fourth-down choice. This can distort field-goal attempts,
punts, scoring, field position, and chart-calibration comparisons across seasons.

## Resolution / verification

Replace the fixed distance tiers with an era-aware decision. Prefer the kicking team's actual
field-goal chart or an engine helper that estimates make probability at the attempted distance,
then combine that probability with score, clock, down-and-distance, field position, and the
season-configured fourth-down aggression. Retain explicit desperation behavior when a field goal
can tie or win.

Add synthetic/public-2026 regressions before changing production code:

- Identical fourth-down situations can produce different kick decisions for weak historical and
  strong modern field-goal charts.
- The season `fourth_down_aggression` setting affects go-for-it versus kick/punt decisions.
- Late-game tie/win field goals retain priority when appropriate.
- Clearly out-of-range attempts remain punts or conversion attempts.
- Easy, Medium, and Hard remain deterministic under controlled RNG.

Run the AI audit and multi-season scoring/field-goal comparison after the fix. Do not tune generated
chart strength to compensate for this coaching defect.

--- 2026-11-15 00:00 ---
Implementation complete in the working tree; not committed or marked resolved
yet. `field_goal_make_probability` in `paydirt/play_resolver.py` reproduces the
penalty-aware kicking procedure without dice: chart rows are weighted by the
216-combination dice distribution, penalty rows reroll and are renormalized
away, and blocks, fumbles, no-good and empty cells are misses. The engine
exposes it for the possession team's chart, and `_fourth_down_decision` uses
four probability bands (0.70 routine, 0.35 worth taking, 0.15 long but
plausible) instead of fixed statistical distances. Effective aggression is
`clamp(season.fourth_down_aggression + difficulty - 0.5, 0, 1)`, so the 1983
era profile is conservative and the 2026 profile aggressive. The 10-second
end-of-half rule and last-second tie/win rule now require the chart's own
probability instead of a fixed 47-yard distance.

Tests first: the weak-vs-strong chart, possession-team ownership,
season-aggression, all-penalty, fallback, and Monte Carlo resolution-parity
regressions are in `tests/test_field_goal_probability.py` and
`tests/test_fourth_down_probability.py`. Two existing expectations changed
because the old fixed tier, not the fixture, was wrong: the 55-yard kick in
`tests/test_fourth_down.py` now punts (the public chart converts it about 6% of
the time), and the 69-yard-chart tying boundary in `tests/test_ai_endgame.py`
now kicks because the chart carries the same mass one yard farther.

Public-only validation, no private charts:
- `tests/run_full_season.py 1983`: 224 regular games and 9 postseason games
  passed using public `seasons/1983` charts.
- `tests/run_full_season.py 2024`: 272 regular games and 13 postseason games
  passed using public `seasons/2024` charts.
- Public 2026 sample AI audit (`--season 2026 --weeks 4 --seed 8306`): every
  difficulty completed 4/4 games with no observational concentration warnings
  and no chart tuning.

Known limit: the probability is the non-penalty continuation. A drawn foul
still produces the engine's ordinary choice among the kick result and the
penalty, and that later choice is not folded into the pre-kick decision.
