## Description

`ComputerAI._fourth_down_decision()` applies fixed 1983 assumptions to every season: an easy kick
is at most 35 yards, a makeable kick is at most 47, and the long-kick boundary is 55. The method's
docstring and comments explicitly describe 1983 coaching and success rates, but a 2024 game uses
the same thresholds even though long-distance kicking improved substantially after 1983.

The decision also uses the difficulty aggression value (`0.3`, `0.5`, or `0.7`) rather than the
season's existing `ai_behavior.strategic.fourth_down_aggression` value. As a result, changing the
era profile does not change this core fourth-down choice. This can distort field-goal attempts,
punts, scoring, field position, and chart-calibration comparisons across seasons.

## Resolution / verification

Replace the fixed distance tiers with an era-aware decision. Prefer the kicking team's actual
field-goal chart or an engine helper that estimates make probability at the attempted distance,
then combine that probability with score, clock, down-and-distance, field position, and the
season-configured fourth-down aggression. Retain explicit desperation behavior when a field goal
can tie or win.

Add synthetic/public-2026 regressions before changing production code:

- Identical fourth-down situations can produce different kick decisions for weak historical and
  strong modern field-goal charts.
- The season `fourth_down_aggression` setting affects go-for-it versus kick/punt decisions.
- Late-game tie/win field goals retain priority when appropriate.
- Clearly out-of-range attempts remain punts or conversion attempts.
- Easy, Medium, and Hard remain deterministic under controlled RNG.

Run the AI audit and multi-season scoring/field-goal comparison after the fix. Do not tune generated
chart strength to compensate for this coaching defect.
