## Description

The independent reviewer found that deleting call/outcome distributions or
changing declared normal offense weights did not invalidate engine evidence.
The former situation key also omitted selected defense and retained no ordered
call receipts or comparable-situation repetition. Aggregate source-fit metrics
do not certify these required policy-sensitivity diagnostics.

## Resolution / verification

Fifteen new public/synthetic cases fail against the unchanged implementation;
49 existing controls pass. Eight mutants remove/corrupt call, outcome, policy or
repetition evidence. Seven cases expose missing ordered decisions, selected
defense and repetition reconstruction. The local correction retains each game's
ordered selected-call/result pairs, validates typed contexts, reconstructs call
and outcome distributions, checks declared offense/defense weights, and reports
per-team/down/distance/mode call shares, distinct calls and longest repetition.
Comparable appearances reset between games and compare offensive choices
regardless of defense; full call/outcome inventory still includes defense.

No concentration becomes an arbitrary frequency cap. This correction does not
establish historically accurate coaching, certify all required stress families,
or substitute for the full readiness bundle. Fresh source candidates, policy
campaigns and independent review remain required. Keep open.
