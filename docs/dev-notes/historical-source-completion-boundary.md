# Historical source completion boundary

Status: November 7, 2026 investigation and decision proposal. The owned
zero-credit field-goal subtype is implemented locally; the remaining missing-data
policy below is proposed, unimplemented and unaccepted. No corrected 1983 cohort
has been generated or qualified by this work.

The complete 224-game source census preserves 727 fumble events. After fourteen
reviewed official-credit corrections and three supported aborted-field-goal
projections, 564 have supported projections, 163 remain held, and no source
conflicts are reported. The previous 561 projections retain their values; their
serialized facts now explicitly include a false aborted-field-goal marker.
Private source documents, review packets and census receipts remain ignored.

The new field-goal tokens require reviewed zero rushing credit and known individual
ownership. They preserve separate fumbling and credited actors and unknown
physical movement. The engine consumes a rushing snap with no kick attempt and no
recovery reroll. A fourth reviewed aborted field goal remains held because the
required credit is unknown. These facts do not establish population completeness
or historical fit.

The remaining work queue contains 54 events requiring exact primary-source
review, 63 reviewed association/action gaps, 45 explicit snaps lacking independently
supported credited class/gain, and the one aborted-field-goal credit gap. These
are investigation categories, not new source facts. Failed OCR is not negative
evidence. Quarterback position, box residuals, loose-ball movement and terminal
recovery position cannot establish a missing originating class or physical gain.

## Proposed bounded decision

Keep the strict source-completeness gate. Do not substitute the previous charts
or a partial prototype for a corrected, qualified 1983 cohort. The recommended
release split is to ship independently reviewed eligible work while holding the
1983 correction for the unresolved evidence.

If a partial historical experiment is desired, obtain an explicit missing-data
policy decision before implementing it. Such a policy must retain all 727 source
events, disclose the 163 unresolved events by team and mechanism, distinguish
supported-sample denominators from full-season denominators, and state whether
unresolved events are excluded from fitting or assigned a declared modeled prior.
Either choice is a new modeling assumption, not recovered historical evidence.
It requires synthetic test-first coverage and a fresh source-fit assessment;
the experiment must remain labeled incomplete and unqualified.

No policy may silently convert an unknown snap to a sack/run, transfer credit
between actors, treat loose-ball loss as initial gain, or widen existing fit
tolerances. Continue exact primary-source review only where a specific page or
independent credited-stat record can resolve a named missing fact. Repeating the
failed OCR pipeline without a changed input or method does not meet that bound.
