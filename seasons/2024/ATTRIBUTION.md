# Generated Chart Attribution

Paydirt includes independent statistical reconstructions for the 1983 and 2024
football seasons. The chart files were produced by this repository's chart
generator and are distributed under the game's MIT License.

## 1983

The 1983 charts were generated from historical play-by-play and aggregate season
data. Play-by-play information was obtained from archived Pro Football Reference
records. No original Paydirt team charts or raw Pro Football Reference data are
included.

## 2024

The 2024 charts were generated from play-by-play and aggregate season data supplied
by the nflverse project. nflverse play-by-play data is provided under the Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0). No raw nflverse data or
original Paydirt team charts are included.

These unofficial charts are not endorsed by Sports Reference, nflverse, the NFL,
Avalon Hill, or the original game designers. Team names and other trademarks belong
to their respective owners.

## Corrected release scope

The corrected 2024 output retains the user-deferred Ravens rushing-fit exception: absolute yards-per-attempt error 0.417273 versus the unchanged 0.4 tolerance. This is not a passing accuracy claim.

Local byte-bound source reproduction proof exists. Public CI checks committed chart structure, loadability and decoder support; it does not regenerate private sources.
