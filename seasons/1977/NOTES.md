# 1977 season

All 28 teams are independent statistical reconstructions generated by Paydirt,
not transcriptions of original Paydirt boards. Historical winners and playoff
qualifiers are never forced. The season uses its 14-game regular calendar and
an eight-team, seven-game simulated postseason; official NFL tiebreaks remain
unimplemented approximations.

## Sources and modeled scope

Charts use reviewed archived Pro Football Reference play-by-play and normalized
season totals, recovered through the Internet Archive. Attribution does not
imply endorsement. No original Paydirt charts, raw archived pages, private
inventories or narrative logs are distributed.

The model retains 50 unresolved fumble-gain exclusions from fitting, 158 modeled
fumble classifications, four conflicting fumble boxes, five exact play-by-play
versus credited-sack differences, and two partial source games. Role/distance
classification assumptions do not establish missing source yardage, recovery or
possession facts. Missing out-of-bounds and selected special-team details use
explicit modeling defaults. Historical source completeness remains false.

Two ordinary generator runs, each with deterministic complete-cohort development
training, reproduced all 84 CSV charts and the full canonical manifest exactly.
The published manifest omits raw input inventories and binds that canonical
manifest by SHA-256. No individual team edits or post-generation corrections
were applied. Global calibration is run gain 1.15, pass gain 1.44, completion +.049,
interception +.004 and pressure 0; probability adjustments use fractions, not
percentage multipliers.
